# Creative Quality Rubric

## Decision model

Creative Quality uses four layers:

1. **Hard Blockers** — factual/product/UI/role/mobile failures that cannot be offset by visual attractiveness.
2. **Asset Quality** — nine structured dimensions totaling 100 points.
3. **Role profile** — Hero, Proof, Lifestyle and other roles have different minimums.
4. **Set/Page context** — individually strong assets can still fail as a repetitive or incoherent page.

The numeric score is a diagnostic/triage aid. It is not an objective aesthetics measurement and does not replace visual/model evaluation or final human approval.

## Asset dimensions

| Dimension | Max |
| --- | ---: |
| Message Clarity | 20 |
| Visual Proof Strength | 15 |
| Shopper Value Translation | 15 |
| Product Prominence & Fidelity | 10 |
| Scene Credibility | 10 |
| Japan Localization | 10 |
| Visual Hierarchy & Composition | 10 |
| Commercial Polish | 5 |
| Mobile Legibility | 5 |

General recommendation requires total >=85 and Message Clarity >=16. Hard Blockers always force `FAIL`.

### Explicit role minimums

- `HERO_POSITIONING`: total >=88, Message Clarity >=18, Product Prominence & Fidelity >=9, Commercial Polish >=4.
- `DIFFERENTIATOR_PROOF`: total >=88, Message Clarity >=17, Visual Proof Strength >=13.
- `LIFESTYLE_USE_CASE`: total >=85, Scene Credibility >=9, Japan Localization >=9, Shopper Value Translation >=13.

Other roles inherit the general thresholds until a stricter profile is explicitly approved.

## Status interpretation

- `FINAL_QUALITY`: >=90 and all applicable minimums pass.
- `PASS`: 85–89 and all applicable minimums pass.
- `REVIEW`: 80–84, or a role/general minimum fails despite a higher total.
- `REVISE`: <80 without a hard blocker.
- `FAIL`: one or more Hard Blockers.

A `PASS`/`FINAL_QUALITY` result means recommendable for human creative review; it is not user approval and not evidence verification.
