# Amazon Japan Localization Profile

Localization is a strategy input to creative quality, not a cosmetic Japan-style filter. The question is whether the product situation, shopper behavior, message and visual treatment are plausible for Japanese consumer life while remaining grounded in project evidence.

## Functional Localization

Check product fit against relevant Japanese usage conditions such as residential scale, entrance/door conditions, outlets, mounting constraints, renter versus detached-home contexts, household composition and other category-specific realities. Do not invent a Japan-specific need without evidence.

## Scene / Behavior Localization

Check whether the depicted situation and human behavior are believable: entrance and delivery behavior, commuting or school-return patterns where relevant, room density, furniture scale, body position, device placement, family interaction and the actual reason the product is needed at that moment.

A Japanese-looking room with implausible behavior is not localized.

## Message / Copy Localization

Translate approved meaning into natural `ja-JP` shopper language. Preserve claim conditions and product truth. Prefer concise mobile-readable headlines, stable terminology, native word order and category language supported by current market/channel evidence. Machine-generated copy remains provisional until native review when the project requires consumer-facing release copy.

## Visual Localization

Avoid obvious non-Japanese residential scale, unsuitable doors or mounting environments, culturally implausible interaction, oversized spaces that distort the use case, and generic AI-stock aesthetics. Visual localization should support the shopper task rather than add decorative Japanese markers.

## Evidence boundary

Stable language conventions may be reused. Volatile search behavior, market preferences, housing assumptions, competitor behavior and channel conventions require current evidence. Localization never changes Product Truth, offer scope, measured performance or claim readiness.
